# Cloud & Capital website content principles

Agreed with Diana on October 19, 2026, while reviewing the value and structure of an educational webinar offer.

Lead with the visitor's question and make the value of each offer visible before asking them to click. A new visitor should understand what it is for, what they do with it, what they get, and why that result helps them.

For tool cards, use a plain-language task, a short description of the action or input, a concrete "What you get" line, a real screenshot, and one clear action. Preserve the screenshot-led layout and consistent links with nearby arrows. Keep explanations brief enough to scan.

Describe actual capabilities and usable outputs. Name a demo as a demo. Do not imply account connections, automatic savings, proven trading returns, or completed functionality that has not been verified. Describe the benefit in ordinary words rather than internal terms such as "evidence-gated route comparison."

Use the same principle for services and future educational offers: show the outcome, the deliverables included, and the next step. The services already include "You leave with" descriptions. A future Chart Basics offer can name the lesson, guide, indicator, worksheet, and paper-trading practice when those items are ready. Work on Sunny's learning kit remains in its existing project.

Keep the homepage to three featured tools. As the collection grows, organize the full gallery by the task a visitor wants to accomplish, retaining screenshots and clear outcomes for each entry.
